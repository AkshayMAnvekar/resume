About

I am an AI and full-stack engineer with more than eight years of experience architecting software systems, enterprise cloud platforms, and automated data pipelines — with a dedicated focus on applied enterprise AI since 2025.

My sweet spot is bridging raw algorithmic research and production software: designing multi-agent workflows, isolated RAG corpora, and LLM fine-tuning pipelines, alongside responsive web frontends and microservices that teams actually depend on daily.

Certified as a Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect, I emphasize security, strict GDPR/privacy governance, single-tenant enterprise isolation, and deterministic observability across complex AI orchestrations.

When I’m not running local quantized models on NVIDIA Jetson hardware or engineering automated pipelines, I enjoy motorcycle riding, traveling, DIY engineering projects, and badminton.
